Statistics & Probability

A structured path from basic probability to inference, ANOVA, correlation, and regression.

Learn → Statistics & Probability

Build the statistical foundation that supports Data Science.

Statistics and Probability allow us to reason about uncertainty, samples, variability, and evidence. This path is designed to move step by step from basic probability concepts to inferential tools used constantly in Data Science.

The order is intentional: first probability and random variables, then distributions, followed by sampling and the Central Limit Theorem, and finally estimation, hypothesis testing, ANOVA, correlation, and regression.

Recommended order: follow the guides in sequence. In particular, keep Sampling Distributions → Central Limit Theorem → Standard Error → Confidence Intervals → Hypothesis Testing together because each topic depends on intuition developed earlier.

Why this order?

The sequence begins with probability because we need a formal way to describe uncertainty before studying random variables and distributions. We then introduce populations, samples, and sampling distributions to understand why a statistic changes from one sample to another.

The Central Limit Theorem connects sampling variability to Standard Error and opens the door to Confidence Intervals and Hypothesis Testing. From there, the path advances to t-tests, F-tests, ANOVA, Chi-Square tests, and finally to correlation and simple linear regression from a statistical perspective.
