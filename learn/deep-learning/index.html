Learn · Deep Learning

Deep Learning

A structured path from tensors and Neural Networks to CNNs, RNNs, Attention, Transformers, PyTorch, and deployment.

Learn → Deep Learning

Understand how Neural Networks learn from first principles.

Deep Learning can feel complex because it combines linear algebra, calculus, optimization, and programming. This path organizes those ideas into a progressive sequence so that each concept appears when the necessary intuition is already in place.

We begin with tensors and the structure of a Neural Network, then study Forward Propagation, Loss Functions, Backpropagation, and optimization. From there, we move into CNNs, sequence models, Attention, Transformers, and practical training with PyTorch.

Recommended order: follow the sequence from Tensors through Deployment. Backpropagation, CNNs, and Transformers become much clearer after you understand tensors, neurons, activation functions, loss, and gradients.

Why this order?

We first build the representation of data with tensors and the structure of neurons and layers. Then we follow the natural flow of a prediction: Forward Propagation → Loss → Backpropagation → Gradient Descent and Optimizers.

With that foundation, we study regularization, normalization, and the problems of vanishing and exploding gradients. We then move into specialized architectures: CNNs for images and RNNs, LSTMs, and GRUs for sequences.

Finally, Attention and Transformers connect the path to modern architectures, while PyTorch and the Deep Learning Lifecycle move the theory toward implementation, inference, monitoring, and deployment.
