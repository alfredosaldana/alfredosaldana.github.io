Mathematics for Data Science

A structured path from vectors and matrices to PCA, SVD, multivariable calculus, and optimization.

Learn → Mathematics for Data Science

Understand the mathematics behind models and algorithms.

Many Data Science tools rely on linear algebra, geometry, and calculus even when those operations are hidden inside a library. This path is designed to make those ideas visible and build them from first principles.

We begin with scalars, vectors, and matrices; move into linear systems, subspaces, projections, eigenvalues, PCA, and SVD; and finally connect those ideas with derivatives, gradients, and optimization.

Recommended order: follow the complete sequence. PCA, SVD, Gradient Descent, and Backpropagation become much clearer after mastering vectors, matrices, projections, and derivatives.

Why this order?

We first build the language of vectors and matrices because much of Data Science represents data using matrix structures. We then study linear systems, span, basis, rank, and linear transformations to understand how information is organized and transformed.

Orthogonality, projections, and Least Squares prepare the ground for Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors, PCA, and SVD—essential tools for dimensionality reduction, compression, and structural analysis.

Finally, functions, derivatives, gradients, the Chain Rule, and convexity connect this foundation with optimization, Gradient Descent, and the mathematical mechanisms used to train Machine Learning and Deep Learning models.
